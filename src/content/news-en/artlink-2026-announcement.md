WESON MUSEUM will hold the group VR art exhibition ARTLINK 2026 on VRChat from Sunday, November 1 to Saturday, November 14, 2026.

ARTLINK is a multi-artist exhibition that has grown out of WESON MUSEUM since 2023. This edition brings together 12 artists. Visitors walk through the venue freely as avatars, and during the run, participating artists will be present in the venue for gallery talks. Hearing directly from an artist in front of the work, and sharing what you noticed with the people around you — we consider that conversation part of the experience of viewing.

## Overview

- **Dates**: Sunday, November 1 – Saturday, November 14, 2026
- **Venue**: ARTLINK2026会場 ⁄ Venue (VRChat)
- **Hours**: Open at any time during the run
- **Admission**: Free
- **Organizer**: WESON MUSEUM / ARTLINK

[Open the venue in VRChat](https://vrchat.com/home/world/wrld_0ac2766e-de64-4add-a890-3e0dab8dce15)

The latest dates, artists, and talk schedule are kept on the [ARTLINK 2026 page](/en/artlink/2026/).
